01 · THE PROBLEM

Real income, illegible to institutions

Delivery partners, drivers, vendors, and freelancers earn reliably every day. But without payslips or bureau scores, that reliability never reaches the systems deciding their access to credit.

Instant rejection

"No bureau history" is treated as high risk by default, closing the door before any real income data is even considered.

Forgeable evidence

PDF statements and screenshots can be edited in minutes, so the documents meant to build trust are the easiest ones to fake.

Invasive alternatives

Some lenders demand apps that scrape contacts and SMS logs — trading one exclusion for a privacy violation.

Self-reinforcing exclusion

No credit history means no approval, and no approval means no way to ever build one. The cycle never breaks on its own.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

From income to credential, in four steps

Earners connect their own accounts. Everything after that happens on-device — lenders see a signed conclusion, never the underlying data.

01

Connect

The earner logs into a gig platform, bank, or utility account through a secure, authenticated session — no PDF uploads, no screenshots.

02

Attest

A cryptographic session proof confirms the data came straight from the real platform server, unedited and untampered with.

03

Compute, on-device

Income, consistency, and repayment metrics are calculated locally. Nothing raw ever leaves the earner's device.

04

Disclose selectively

The earner shares a signed credential — a threshold that's true or false — and the lender verifies it in milliseconds.
